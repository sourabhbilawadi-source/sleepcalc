The Science of "Beauty Sleep"

How deep sleep acts as a natural biological anti-aging treatment.

1. Human Growth Hormone (HGH) Peak

During stage 3 deep sleep (typically occurring in the first few sleep cycles), your pituitary gland secretes a massive pulse of HGH. This hormone is essential for repairing skin cells, synthesizing proteins, and restoring cellular integrity.

2. Cortisol Regulation

Restful sleep lowers systemic cortisol levels. High cortisol (the stress hormone) directly disrupts collagen production, drys out the epidermal barrier, and can trigger inflammatory flares like eczema or acne.

3. Microcirculation and Hydration

During deep sleep, the body increases blood flow to the face. This delivers fresh oxygen, clears metabolic waste, and allows moisture levels to rebalance, preventing puffiness and under-eye dark circles.
