Best Magnesium Supplements for Sleep

The neuroscience of GABAergic minerals. Compare glycinate, threonate, and citrate, and identify your optimal magnesium form.

Sleep Science June 19, 2026 ⏱️ 5 min read

Magnesium is a vital mineral involved in over 300 biochemical reactions in the human body. Clinically, it acts as a primary coordinator of sleep architecture by operating as an agonist for **GABA (gamma-aminobutyric acid)**—the neurotransmitter responsible for down-regulating central nervous system activity. Magnesium also binds to NMDA receptors, inhibiting the release of excitatory neurotransmitters like glutamate [1].

However, when buying magnesium at the store, you face a confusing list of chemical formulations. The molecule that magnesium is bound to (the chelate) completely determines its absorption rate, bioavailability, and side effects. This guide evaluates the best magnesium supplements for sleep.

Comparing Magnesium Formulations

1. Magnesium Glycinate - The Gold Standard

Magnesium bound to the amino acid **glycine**. Glycine acts as an inhibitory neurotransmitter in its own right, promoting calm and helping lower your core body temperature. This combination has exceptional bioavailability, is highly gentle on the stomach, and is the clinical choice for general sleep improvement.

2. Magnesium L-Threonate - Best for Cognitive Calming

A specialized, patented compound that crosses the **blood-brain barrier** far more efficiently than other forms. It increases cerebrospinal fluid levels of magnesium, boosting synaptic plasticity and reducing racing thoughts. It is ideal for individuals whose insomnia is triggered by stress or anxiety [2].

3. Magnesium Citrate - Generally Avoid for Sleep

Magnesium bound to citric acid. It has moderate bioavailability but operates as an **osmotic laxative**, drawing water into the intestines. Taking therapeutic sleep dosages of magnesium citrate can lead to abdominal cramping and night-time bathroom trips, disrupting your sleep cycles.

4. Magnesium Oxide - Poor Absorption

An inorganic form with a tiny absorption rate (about 4%). While cheap, most of the magnesium passes unabsorbed through your GI tract, leading to diarrhea without improving sleep architecture.

Dosage and Safety Protocols

For sleep support, clinical studies suggest the following guidelines:

  • Magnesium Glycinate: Take **200mg to 400mg** of elemental magnesium 1 hour before bed.
  • Magnesium L-Threonate: Take **1,000mg to 2,000mg** (providing about 144mg of elemental magnesium) 1 to 2 hours before bed.
  • Kidney Warning: If you have impaired kidney function, consult your physician before supplementing, as your kidneys are responsible for clearing excess magnesium from the bloodstream.
