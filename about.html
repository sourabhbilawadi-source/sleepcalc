Our Principles

  • Science-first: Every recommendation is backed by published research. We cite our sources and avoid pseudoscience.
  • Free forever: All tools are completely free to use. No premium tiers, no paywalls, no sign-ups required.
  • Privacy-respecting: We don't collect personal data. Your sleep calculations stay in your browser.
  • No medical claims: We provide educational information, not medical advice. Always consult a healthcare professional for persistent sleep issues.

Sources & References

Our content draws from established sleep research, including:

  • National Sleep Foundation (NSF) sleep duration recommendations
  • American Academy of Sleep Medicine (AASM) clinical guidelines
  • Matthew Walker's research on sleep cycles and circadian rhythms
  • CBT-I research from the American College of Physicians
  • NASA napping studies on alertness and performance
